# Licensing and third-party material

The root MIT license applies to original BryceCast software and original documentation, including the deliberately synthetic examples. It does **not** relicense third-party source data, normalized source records, generated data packages, photographs, logos, broadcast material, or track-map source material. Inclusion in this private candidate does not establish public redistribution permission.

- Data/source rights: [DATA_RELEASE.md](DATA_RELEASE.md) and [permission outreach](docs/PERMISSIONS_OUTREACH.md).
- Historical permissions evidence: [project ledger](docs/DATA_PERMISSIONS.md) and [source-use record](data/historical-data-lake/PERMISSIONS_AND_SOURCE_TERMS.md). These record historical scope; they do not grant a new public-release right.
- OpenStreetMap-derived geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Preserve attribution and evaluate database share-alike obligations for each geometry bundle.
- Natural Earth material: retain the [source record](analysis/career-atlas/data/SOURCE.md).
- Open-Meteo: attribute its [CC BY 4.0 data](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms); API-service terms and data licensing are distinct.
- NWS/NOAA and other weather providers: retain source-specific notices and origin, even where the data is public domain.
- Dependency packages retain their own licenses. npm installs them from the lockfile; they are not vendored as BryceCast-authored code. Python analytics dependencies are recorded in `analysis/requirements.txt`.

Do not infer that permission from one archive operator grants rights in all underlying series data. Do not infer endorsement by a racing series, team, broadcaster, or data provider. The eventual data releases will carry per-family permission receipts and licenses rather than a blanket MIT label.
